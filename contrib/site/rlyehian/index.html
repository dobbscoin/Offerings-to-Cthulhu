fhtagn means wait, and sleep, and dream — the Old Ones experience these as one condition, and a translator that keeps only the first has already lied to you. Words we cannot source are left standing, marked, untranslated: better an honest gap than confident nonsense. Verbs have only present and not-present tense; taken out of context, any fragment's translation is guesswork — this page shows you the guesswork instead of hiding it.
